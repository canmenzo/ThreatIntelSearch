Everything here is optional. Without keys the panel still shows every lookup link. With keys it also fills the score circle, using your own free vendor accounts.

Where your data goes

  • Keys stay in this browser's extension storage. They are only ever sent to the vendor they belong to.
  • A score is fetched only when you open a lookup, straight from this extension to the vendor's API. No cookies are sent.
  • Results are cached locally for 30 minutes so repeat lookups don't burn your free quota.
  • Nothing is sent to the extension's author. There is no server, analytics, or telemetry.
  • The score is a convenience heuristic, not a verdict.
