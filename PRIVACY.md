# Privacy Policy — Threat Intel Search

Last updated: 2026-08-02

Threat Intel Search does **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell any user data.

- The extension has no servers, no analytics, and no telemetry.
- It requests a single permission, `contextMenus`, which is used only to add the
  right-click menu entries.
- It does not read page content. The only data it touches is the text you have
  selected, at the moment you click a menu item, and it is used solely to build
  the lookup URL that is opened in a new tab.
- Nothing is written to disk or to browser storage.

Opening a lookup sends your selected text to the third-party service you chose
(VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB, AlienVault OTX, or MaxMind), exactly as if you had typed
it into that site yourself. Their handling of that request is governed by their
own privacy policies.

Questions or concerns: https://github.com/canmenzo/ThreatIntelSearch/issues
